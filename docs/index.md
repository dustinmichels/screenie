    details: When you take a screenshot, a dot appears in the center of the circle. Opening the popover clears it and highlights the new shot.
  - title: Open, copy, reveal, trash, drag
    details: Double-click to open, hover for quick buttons, right-click for the full menu, or drag a thumbnail into another app.
  - title: Cheap to leave running
    details: Event-driven, with no polling. In testing it sat at 0% CPU and about 14 MB of memory with the popover closed.
